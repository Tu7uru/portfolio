BSc thesis: Elasticsearch, Solr, and Algolia evaluated on Greek documents, with a typo-tolerant query correction algorithm.

- Three search engines evaluated on a hand-built Greek dataset of 57 queries and 118 documents with human relevance judgments.
- Measured with precision, recall, F-measure, and fall-out.
- The correction algorithm models the Greek keyboard layout and repairs mistypes caused by accidental adjacent key presses.
- It combines keyboard distance with edit distance.
