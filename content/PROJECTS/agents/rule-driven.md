Global, technology-neutral design rules, enforced by deterministic checks so AI agents build within one consistent system design.

- Rules reach from architecture and methodology down to code.
- Rule books and workflows define the expected output, and the agent refines its work until deterministic scripts confirm it meets them.
- Deterministic validation is preferred over AI review because AI review does not give reproducible verdicts.
